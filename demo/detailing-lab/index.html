Questions

Before you book

How do I know which package I need?
Send a couple of photos through the form or WhatsApp and we'll recommend the right package for your car's condition — no obligation, no upsell.
Do you guarantee the results?
Yes — we're fully insured and we don't leave until you're happy. If something isn't right, we make it right.
How long will the ceramic coating last?
With sensible maintenance washing, our ceramic coatings protect and bead for years, not months. We'll give you a simple aftercare guide.
Do you really come to me?
Yes — we're fully mobile across Yorkshire with our own power and water, or you can book into the indoor studio for correction and ceramic work.

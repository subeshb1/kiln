Questions, answered honestly

FAQ

Does it have a screen?

No — on purpose. A screen on your wrist becomes another feed. Meridian's job is to gather signals silently and give you one considered answer each morning in the app. If you want a watch, keep wearing your watch; Meridian sits on the other wrist and disappears.

Really no subscription?

Really. $299 buys the hardware and every metric it can measure, forever. We'd rather sell you a strap in two years than rent you your own heart rate.

Is the 10-day battery honest?

It's our measured median across the test fleet with all-night sleep tracking on. Heavy workout logging brings it closer to 8 days; airplane mode stretches it past 12. We publish the test protocol.

What happens to my data?

Scores are computed on the band and your phone. Cloud sync is off by default, opt-in for backup only, and export or full deletion is one tap. We don't sell data — there's no subscription business model to feed.

How do I get the right size?

Three strap sizes cover wrists from 135 mm to 220 mm. A printable sizing guide arrives with your waitlist confirmation, and swaps are free within 60 days.

Nervous patients

Gentle by design, not by slogan.

About a third of our patients arrive anxious. These aren't perks — they're standard procedure at Lumen.

Numb first, always

Topical numbing gel before any needle, and we wait until it's working — not until we're ready.

The hand rule

Raise your hand and everything stops. Immediately, every time, no questions. You're in charge of the pace.

Noise-cancelling headphones

Bring your own playlist or borrow ours. Most people say the sound is worse than the feeling — so we remove it.

Sedation options

For longer treatment, IV sedation with a dedicated sedationist. You'll remember almost none of it.

Your first visit

What actually happens.

STEP 01

Talk first, chair later

We start in a normal room, sitting in normal chairs. Tell us your history, your worries, and what you want — or don't want — to happen today.

STEP 02

Look, then explain

A gentle exam and photos of your own teeth on the big screen. You see what we see. Nothing is done without you agreeing to it.

STEP 03

A written plan with real prices

You leave with a plan, exact costs, and zero obligation. Some people book treatment that day; some think for a month. Both are fine.

Honest answers

The questions people actually ask.

Does it hurt?

Sometimes dentistry involves discomfort — we won't pretend otherwise. What we promise: you'll be properly numb before anything sharp, you can stop us at any moment, and we'll tell you honestly what each step feels like before it happens. Most patients say the fear was worse than the visit.

I haven't been in years. Will I get judged?

No. Around a third of our new patients haven't seen a dentist in five-plus years. You'll get a plan, not a lecture — we're glad you came, and we'll say so.

How much will my first visit cost?

£65 for the new-patient exam, including X-rays and photos. If you need treatment, you'll get exact written prices before anything is booked. Emergencies are £95 including immediate relief.

Do you take NHS patients?

We're a private practice, so no — but our prices are published, our plans are itemised, and 0% finance spreads anything over £500 across 12 months. No hidden costs is the whole point of how we work.

What if I'm too anxious to even book?

Book a free 15-minute chat instead — no chair, no exam, just a conversation with Dr. Whitlow about what would make a visit possible for you. Several patients started with two or three of these.

Adaptive ANC

Hear nothing.
Or everything.

Eight microphones map the noise around you and a dedicated neural chip cancels it — up to 48 dB deep, retuned every five milliseconds. Tap once and Transparency lifts the world back in, voice-forward and natural.

Cancellation that adapts to the carriage, the cabin, the café.

Most ANC is tuned once, in a lab. Nocturne One retunes continuously — pressure changes on a flight, the low rumble of a train, a sudden espresso grinder — each profile computed on-device, never in the cloud.

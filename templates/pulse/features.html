Features

Everything you need. Nothing you have to babysit.

Pulse is deliberately small: five capabilities that answer 95% of product questions, each switched on by default the moment the snippet is installed.

01 · Autocapture

Tracking plans are a tax. Stop paying it.

Every click, pageview, form submit, and route change becomes a named event automatically. Rename them later; the history is already there.

  • Retroactive — ask questions about last month even if you defined the event today
  • Selector-smart — survives class-name churn and framework rewrites
  • One snippet — nothing to instrument, nothing to forget

See exactly where they give up.

  • Click a drop-off to watch the replays behind it
  • Anomaly alerts when a step's conversion shifts by more than 2σ

03 · Session replays

The number says what. The replay says why.

Every metric links to the sessions behind it. Pulse pre-flags the moments worth watching so you skip the scrubbing.

  • Rage clicks, dead clicks, and u-turns detected automatically
  • Inputs masked by default — replays are privacy-safe out of the box
  • Share a moment with a timestamped link, not a screen recording

Compliant by design, not by banner.

Pulse runs entirely on EU infrastructure and never uses cookies or cross-site identifiers — which is why most customers don't need a consent banner for it at all.

  • Cookieless by architecture; IPs anonymized at ingest

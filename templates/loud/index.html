The manifesto

Read this out loud.

  • Safe is expensive. Blending in costs more than any agency ever will — you just pay for it in being forgotten.
  • Taste is a strategy. “Data-driven” and “gorgeous” are not opposites. We ship both or we don’t ship.
  • Funny is serious work. Making someone feel something in 1.5 seconds is the hardest brief in business.
  • Loud ≠ shouting. Loud is being impossible to mistake for anyone else — at any volume.

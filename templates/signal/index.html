Venue

Funkhaus, on the Spree.

East Berlin's old radio complex: timber halls, river terrace, and enough studios to run workshops without shouting over each other.

  • Nalepastraße 18, 12459 Berlin. Tram 21 or M17 to Köpenicker Chaussee, then a five-minute river walk.
  • Doors 08:30 both days. Talks start 09:30 sharp — Berlin traffic is real, plan for it.
  • Hotels: stay around Ostkreuz or Friedrichshain — 15 minutes door to door, and better food.
